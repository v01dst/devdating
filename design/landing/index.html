why devdating?

Most developers want to contribute to open source, but they stall before writing any code. Which project? Is this issue actually beginner-friendly? Is the repo even alive?

DevDating solves discovery first. It scans real repositories and real issues, scores them against your profile, and serves them one swipe at a time. No random awesome lists, no guessing. Just a short path to your first merged pr.
